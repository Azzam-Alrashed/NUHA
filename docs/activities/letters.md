# «كتابة الحروف وتوصيل الكلمات» (`letters`)

Status: draft, written by the developer from the SRS and the built activity (milestone 3). Not reviewed by an educator; every Arabic line below is draft (`@DraftArabic` in code).

## Learning objective
Recognise and form early Arabic letters and link them to words.

## Prerequisites
None for recognition; can hold a stylus or finger for formation.

## Successful behaviour
Formation: a trace covering the guide within tolerance (coverage ≥ 0.8, stray ≤ 0.3, tolerance 0.06 — PLACEHOLDERs). Recognition, missing letter and word–picture: tap the right card.

## Likely confounds
The draft letter guides (hand-made, not educator-approved stroke order); vowel marks; picture style.

## Examples
Letters and words (draft, OI-09): ب بَيْت, ش شَمْس, ق قَمَر, س سَمَكَة, ت تُفَّاحَة, ن نَجْمَة. Tracing guides exist for ب, ت, ن only.
Each session rotates formation, recognition, missing letter, word, recognition. Missing-letter instruction is generic «أيّ حرف ينقص في الكلمة؟» so it does not reveal the answer.

## Accessibility alternative
Formation needs drawing; the other three types are tap only.

## Difficulty factors
Letters in play 3 → 5 → 6.

## What is recorded
Formation strokes and every failed attempt; detail `coverage;stray`.

## Transfer question
For the educator to set.
