# «تلوين المشاعر» (`emotions`)

Status: draft, written by the developer from the SRS and the built activity (milestone 3). Not reviewed by an educator; every Arabic line below is draft (`@DraftArabic` in code).

## Learning objective
Name a basic feeling from a face.

## Prerequisites
Looks at a face on screen; knows or is learning the four words. No reading: every word is spoken.

## Successful behaviour
Taps the word card that matches the buddy's face. The first choice is recorded; a wrong card shakes and the ladder steps up.

## Likely confounds
Vocabulary (the child knows the feeling but not the word); the stylised face; colour is never the cue (feelings differ by brows, eyes and mouth only).
Note: the SRS name says «تلوين» (colouring) but the built task is word matching; see the milestone 3 report.

## Examples
Feelings (draft): سَعِيد «يبتسم.», حَزِين «فمه إلى الأسفل وحاجباه منخفضان.», غَاضِب «حاجباه مقطّبان.», خَائِف «عيناه واسعتان وفمه مفتوح.»
Instruction «كيف يشعر صديقنا؟»; praise «أحسنت! صديقنا <word>، <cue>»; model «صديقنا <word>. <cue> اضغط على «<word>».». Characters rotate over the six shape buddies.

## Accessibility alternative
Tap only. With voice off, each card shows its word in Naskh and a face glyph.

## Difficulty factors
Feelings in play 3 → 4 (level 1), then choices 3 → 4 (level 2).

## What is recorded
Shared trial fields only; item key `<feeling>-<character>`.

## Transfer question
For the educator to set.
