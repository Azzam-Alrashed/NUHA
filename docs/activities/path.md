# «تتبع مسار الأشكال الاجتماعية» (`path`)

Status: draft, written by the developer from the SRS and the built activity (milestone 3). Not reviewed by an educator; every Arabic line below is draft (`@DraftArabic` in code).

## Learning objective
Plan and draw one continuous route between two points around obstacles. Route planning and motor practice, not a measure of social skill.

## Prerequisites
Can draw a line with a finger or stylus.

## Successful behaviour
One stroke that starts at the buddy, ends at its friend and touches no rock. Ends must be within 0.16 of the board's short side.

## Likely confounds
Fine motor control; the friendly characters may suggest a social meaning the task does not measure.

## Examples
Instruction «ارسم طريقًا من صديقنا إلى صديقه، وابتعد عن الصخور.»; praise «أحسنت! وصل صديقنا إلى صديقه بأمان.»; model «انظر، نمرّ بجانب الصخرة ولا نلمسها.» The first trial of each session shows a dashed example route.

## Accessibility alternative
Not yet defined: the task is drawing by nature. Needs a decision (for example, tapping stepping stones).

## Difficulty factors
Rocks 1 → 2 (route complexity), then rock size 0.16 → 0.22 (corridor width).

## What is recorded
Strokes of every attempt; `misses` = rock contacts; detail `contacts;restarts`.

## Transfer question
For the educator to set.
