# «الميزان الحسابي البصري» (`balance`)

Status: draft, written by the developer from the SRS and the built activity (milestone 3). Not reviewed by an educator; every Arabic line below is draft (`@DraftArabic` in code).

## Learning objective
Join two small quantities and find the total that balances them.

## Prerequisites
Counts dots to 8; recognises the numerals in play (OI-02).

## Successful behaviour
Picks the number that balances the scale; the empty pan fills with that many dots and the beam levels. Tapping a dot group says its count and is not an answer.

## Likely confounds
Numeral recognition; counting dots versus adding.

## Examples
Instruction «كم نضع في الكفّة الفارغة ليتوازن الميزان؟»; praise «أحسنت! a و b يساوي sum. الميزان متوازن.»; model «عُدّ النقاط: a و b يساوي sum. اضغط على sum.»

## Accessibility alternative
Tap only. Writing the answer is PLACEHOLDER: it needs offline digit recognition and OI-02.

## Difficulty factors
Sums to 5 → to 8, then choices 3 → 4.

## What is recorded
Shared fields; difficulty records `input=select`.
